- No tests, lint task, lockfile, ignore rules or production images.
- LeadForm reported success on any iframe load, including error pages. Apps Script persisted then redirected to `/cam-on/`.

- Wardrobe, shoe cabinet, TV cabinet and shelving generate estimated BOMs; remaining models explicitly retain provisional sheet assumptions pending construction data. Wood grain, edging, joints, loads, anchoring and purchase prices require workshop confirmation.
- 9 catalog groups, 20 variants; original SKUs retained; new shelving SKUs added. Exact cost × 1.30 × 2 pricing retained.
- Scoped confirmation messages, request IDs, timeout/error UI, duplicate submit lock, accessible 5-second modal. Existing endpoint unchanged. Apps Script source adds persistent idempotency, compatible sheet-column migration and whitespace-safe formula sanitization.
- Legacy success redirect bridge supported; iframe load itself cannot trigger success. Direct thank-you visits cannot emit ad conversions.
- 10 original AI images, responsive WebP variants, visible illustration labels; no claims that imagery is real work.
- Responsive catalog, room links, shared product page, mobile navigation, readable Roboto, labels and focus handling.
- Canonicals, useful guidance, Product/Breadcrumb JSON-LD without fake offers/reviews, Organization schema without unverified workshop-address claims, stable sitemap entries.
